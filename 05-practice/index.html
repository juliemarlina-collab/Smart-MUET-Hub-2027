VAULT / PRACTICE GATEWAY

Ready to put it to work?

Learn it.
Now try it.

Start with a guided lab for each paper: see a model (I do), practise with support (We do), then try a fresh task on your own (You do). Every lab ends with a reflection.

01 / START WITH A LAB

Your four Lab folders.

Each Lab shows a model (I do), practises with you (We do), then gives you a fresh task (You do). Tap a folder to see how to try it.

01 / LAB · 800/1

ListeningCLUE Lab

↗

Predict, catch signposts and eliminate distractors. Device-voice audio with two-play exam mode and transcripts.

Tips & tricks
  1. Read the questions first and predict the type of answer: a time, a reason, an opinion.
  2. First play: get the gist. Second play: confirm the detail.
  3. When you miss one, name the trap: changed plan, partial match or wrong speaker.
Enter the CLUE Lab ↗
02 / LAB · 800/2

SpeakingPREP Lab

↗

Explore a model, build a response and speak on a fresh topic with a timer and optional recording.

Tips & tricks
  1. Plan keywords, not a script. Four words can carry a full PREP answer.
  2. Record once, listen back, then record again with one fix.
  3. Keep to one idea and develop it fully within 2 minutes.
Enter the PREP Lab ↗
03 / LAB · 800/3

ReadingSCAN Lab

↗

Hunt for evidence, spot paraphrases and answer five questions on a fresh text against the clock.

Tips & tricks
  1. Read the question before the passage.
  2. Look for the same meaning in different words, not the same word.
  3. Prove every answer with one line of evidence before you choose.
Enter the SCAN Lab ↗
04 / LAB · 800/4

WritingPEEL Lab

↗

Build a PEEL paragraph, plan a Task 1 email or Task 2 essay, and draft with a timer and word count.

Tips & tricks
  1. Spend 5 minutes planning before you draft.
  2. One PEEL paragraph for each idea; no lists of points.
  3. Check your word count and your Link sentence before you finish.
Enter the PEEL Lab ↗
02 / THEN THE VAULT

Three practice levels.

After the Labs, move from guided practice to exam conditions. Every level is open; the tag shows what we recommend next.

LEVEL 1 · WE DO

Vault 1Guided

↗

Technique prompts and explained feedback help you practise with support.

Tips & tricks
  1. Keep the technique prompts open while you work.
  2. Use a hint before you check, not after.
  3. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right.
Open Vault 1 ↗
LEVEL 2 · YOU DO TOGETHER

Vault 2Less support

↗

Apply the same techniques with fewer prompts and greater independence.

Tips & tricks
  1. Set your own timer for each part.
  2. Answer a whole part before you check anything.
  3. Compare your score with Vault 1: which paper grew most?
Open Vault 2 ↗
LEVEL 3 · YOU DO ALONE

Mock 1Exam conditions

↗

Full papers with official timings and no coaching, then review and reflect.

Tips & tricks
  1. Do one paper in one sitting, phone on silent.
  2. Follow the real timing; do not pause.
  3. Next day, clear the questions saved in Review again.
Open Mock 1 ↗

04 / HOW TO USE THE VAULT

A clear order.
Steady progress.

Follow these four habits and every visit moves your band forward.

01 / ORDER

Lab first,
then Vault.

Learn the technique in the Lab before you use it in a full paper.

02 / PACE

One paper
a day.

Short, regular practice beats one long weekend session.

03 / REVIEW

Mistakes come back.

Missed questions return in 3 days. Clear them before new work.

04 / CHECK

Mock to
measure.

Use Mock 1 to check your level, not to learn the technique.

All three levels are open. “Recommended next” glows on the next level once the earlier one shows 4/4; it never locks a level. Personal progress only, with no class leaderboard.
