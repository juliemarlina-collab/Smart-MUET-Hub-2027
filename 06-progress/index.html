The text export includes guide progress, PDF reading records and all four lab records (plans, drafts, answers, reflections) plus your review queue. Recordings stay in the Speaking Lab and must be downloaded there separately. XP, streaks and badges are worked out from these records on this device. Practice bands are worked out from complete sets of four Vault or Mock papers; they are estimates, not official results. Cross-device syncing is not active in this build.
