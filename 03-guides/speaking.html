01 / KNOW YOUR PURPOSE

Speak clearly.
Connect naturally.

Practise both an individual response and a conversation. These activities develop different speaking habits.

01

Individual
presentation

Develop one clear idea logically.

  1. Read the task carefully.
  2. Plan keywords, rather than a full script.
  3. Explain your point with a reason and an example.
02

Group
discussion

Listen, respond and help move the conversation forward.

  1. Listen before taking your turn.
  2. Build on or challenge ideas politely.
  3. Compare suggestions and work towards a conclusion.

03 / KEEP THE CONVERSATION GOING

Good ideas.
Useful words.

Choose a purpose. Read the phrase aloud, then change it to fit your own idea.

Invite someone in +

“What do you think about this suggestion?”

Try it: invite a quieter member to share a view.

Build on an idea +

“Building on that point, we could also…”

Try it: add a different benefit or example.

Disagree politely +

“I see your point. However, we should also consider…”

Try it: acknowledge the idea before explaining a concern.

Compare and conclude +

“Both ideas are useful, but the first may be more practical because…”

“Shall we agree that…?”
